Before You Write In

Common Questions

How long does a made-to-order pair take?

Standard sizes ship in 5–7 business days. Anything outside our stocked size run is cut to order and takes 3–4 weeks on the bench.

Do you offer resoling on shoes bought elsewhere?

We resole LAST & CO pairs for free within the first 12 months, and at a flat rate after that. We don't currently resole other brands' shoes — the welt construction varies too much to guarantee the fit.

Can I visit the workshop without an appointment?

The showroom floor is open Tuesday–Saturday, 10am–6pm, no appointment needed. Fittings with a maker do need a booking, since only one or two people are free from the bench at a time.

What's your return policy?

Unworn pairs in original packaging can be returned within 30 days. Made-to-order sizes are final sale, since they're cut specifically for you.
