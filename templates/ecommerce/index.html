The Order of Operations

Four stages. No shortcuts.

This is the literal sequence every pair follows on the bench — not a metaphor, the actual build order.

01 / Last

Carve the last

A wooden form is shaped to the pattern's exact measurements — the mold every other step is built around.

02 / Upper

Cut & stitch the upper

Full-grain hide is cut by hand, then closed on the last with saddle-stitching along every seam.

03 / Sole

Weld the sole

A Goodyear welt joins the upper to an oak-tanned leather sole, built to be resoled, not replaced.

04 / Finish

Burnish & box

Edges are burnished by hand, the leather is fed and buffed, and the pair rests for 48 hours before shipping.
